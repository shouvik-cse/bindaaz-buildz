Questions People Ask

How much does a gaming PC cost in Bangladesh right now?

A PC that plays esports games at 1080p starts around ৳70,000. A solid 1080p high-settings build with an RTX 5060 or RX 9060 XT costs about ৳90,000–1,10,000. 1440p gaming with an RTX 5070 or RX 9070 XT starts around ৳1,60,000. RAM and SSD prices are high in 2026. That is why the builder starts you on 16 GB and 512 GB and lets you add more.

Where do the prices come from?

From the product pages of Star Tech, Ryans and Tech Land, read automatically several times a day. Each part shows which shop the price is from, with a link. Always confirm the price and warranty with the shop before buying.

Does the build include a monitor, keyboard or mouse?

No. Every build covers the processor, graphics card, motherboard, RAM, SSD, power supply, case and CPU cooler. Monitor, keyboard, mouse and Windows licence are extra.

Will all the parts work together?

Yes. The builder checks the CPU socket against the motherboard, DDR4 or DDR5 memory, power supply wattage, cooler capacity and case size. It also picks a motherboard of the right class for the processor. A Ryzen 7 never sits on an entry-level board.
